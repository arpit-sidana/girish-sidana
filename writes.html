On the human side of financial decisions, and the lessons that experience leaves us.

Book · September 2026

Behavioural Finance for Everyday Investors

The market collapse of 2008 prompted me to look more closely at investing. I had avoided the worst largely because I had invested little. Recognising the role of luck set me on a long journey to understand the decisions behind financial outcomes.

This book explores how emotional blind spots and mental shortcuts influence investors, and offers a practical framework for more thoughtful decisions. It brings together behavioural finance, psychology, and lessons from market thinkers.
