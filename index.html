Girish Sidana

My journey has taken me from engineering and technology businesses to education, mentoring, and writing. Across these chapters, I’ve been drawn to understanding people, making thoughtful decisions, and turning ideas into something useful.

I’ve led businesses, built partnerships, and worked across technology, geospatial information, and entrepreneurship. Today, as a trustee of Asha Education Trust, I contribute to practical skill education and pathways to employment for young people, with an emphasis on dignity and self-reliance.

I also help individuals make sense of personal finance. My book, Behavioural Finance for Everyday Investors, grew from a long interest in how our minds shape our financial decisions. Through my work and mentoring, I hope to pass on experience that helps others find clarity, confidence, and a path of their own.
